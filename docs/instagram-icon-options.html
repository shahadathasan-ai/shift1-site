Instagram profile icon — options

Instagram shows your avatar at about 32px in feed and comments. That, not the profile page, is the size that decides whether a mark works. Every option below is rendered at the real display sizes so you can judge it where it actually lives — the big circle is a detail view, not the test.

320px
What Instagram stores. Upload at 1024px square and let it downscale.
110–150px
Profile page header. The only place a mark gets room.
56px
Story ring, DM list, suggested accounts.
32px
Feed byline and comments. The size that matters most.
Light surface swaps the accent to a deeper gold — #e8d5a3 is invisible on cream. Export gives a 1024px square; Instagram applies the circle mask itself.
