Shuffle

Horror / Short film / 9 min 15 sec / Based on a true story

Some night shifts are quieter than others. For one laboratory worker, the silence is only the beginning.

9 min 15 sec 1920 × 1080 Contains flashing images
Background

About this piece

This was made for a Higgsfield competition. It puts a short portion of the story on screen rather than the whole thing, and it was also a way to test what the AI tools can actually do.

The story

It is a real story first

The script came first

Shuffle is based on a true story, and the screenplay was finished long before any of this was a video. It was never a technical exercise with a narrative bolted on afterwards to give it a shape.

That ordering matters. The story set the brief, and the tools either met it or they did not.

It is still being made properly

Shuffle is in development as a live-action short, with Tony Lew as director of photography. That film is still the plan, and this is not a replacement for it.

What this version gave us was a way to see the story standing up — to watch it play at length, with sound, and learn what the script was actually doing before a camera ever gets pointed at anything.

The part we were really watching

Our industry is going to be changed by this technology, whether or not anyone in it votes for that. We would rather understand the change from the inside than argue about it from a distance.

So this was an opportunity taken deliberately: make a real thing, under real constraints, and pay attention to what got easier, what got harder, and what still cannot be done at all.

Built from source

The opening title sequence is generated rather than assembled. Every frame comes from a script, and the whole soundtrack under it was synthesised procedurally — oscillators, damped resonators, a seeded random number generator and filters. No samples, no stock library, no third-party audio.

Both generators are committed to this site's repository, so every frame and every sample is reproducible from source.
