Cover / thumbnail — composition studies

Four 16:9 concepts as value and framing studies: where the light falls, how much of the frame is black, how big the figure reads, where the eye lands. No typography, no creature, no blood.

These are not photographs and are not trying to be. They are blocking diagrams — the thing a director and DP compare before committing. Use them to choose a composition, then shoot it or hand the chosen one to a photoreal pass as a precise brief. Judging a fake-photoreal render here would tell you nothing true about the final image.
Squint = blur. If the composition still reads blurred, the silhouette is strong enough for a gallery grid.
