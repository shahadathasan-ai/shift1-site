Everything here is synthesized in the browser sample by sample — the same maths as the final render, so nothing is sampled or downloaded. A real light switch is two transients: the mechanism trips, then the toggle hits its end stop 10–20 ms later. That double impulse is what the earlier attempts were missing. Body & tone

Gap is the interval between the two impulses — set it to 0 for a single tick like the original. Second hit is how loud the end-stop is relative to the trip. Body is the plastic housing resonance: a switch has some, a relay has more, and too much becomes a thud. High-pass keeps it dry — below about 200 Hz you start to get thump, which the brief rules out.
