SHUFFLE — opening title, fluorescent ignition

Eight treatments on pure black in a 2.39:1 frame. Every version runs the same physical model of an old magnetic-ballast tube: cathode pre-glow, partial strikes that light sections of the word rather than the whole word dimly, irregular failures, a long dead beat, then full ignition with overshoot, a held stable burn, and a hard electrical cut. Timing is identical across options so you are comparing typography and light, not choreography.
